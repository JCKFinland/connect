# CONNECT

> **The Smart, Secure & Compliant Taxi Mobility Platform for Finland**

---

## Overview

CONNECT is a next-generation taxi ride-hailing and fleet management platform designed specifically for the Finnish transportation industry.

Unlike conventional ride-hailing applications, CONNECT is being engineered with regulatory compliance, operational transparency, security, and scalability as core design principles. The platform is intended to support taxi operators, independent drivers, fleet owners, dispatchers, and passengers through a modern digital ecosystem.

The long-term objective is to build one of Finland's most reliable, secure, and regulation-aware mobility platforms.

---

# Vision

To provide a safe, transparent, and intelligent taxi mobility platform that benefits passengers, drivers, fleet operators, and transportation authorities through modern software engineering and regulatory compliance.

---

# Project Goals

* Build a production-grade taxi ride-hailing platform.
* Support customers, drivers, fleet operators, and dispatchers.
* Follow Finnish transportation regulations and industry best practices.
* Maintain high standards for security, privacy, and reliability.
* Deliver a scalable architecture capable of supporting future expansion.
