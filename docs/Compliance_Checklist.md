# CONNECT Compliance Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist maps platform capabilities to legal, regulatory, security, and operational obligations. It will be updated throughout development.

---

# Transportation

* Driver licence verification
* Vehicle verification
* Operator verification
* Fare transparency
* Trip record retention
* Driver identification
* Passenger receipts

---

# Privacy

* GDPR data minimization
* User consent management
* Right of access
* Right to rectification
* Right to erasure (where applicable)
* Data portability
* Privacy notices

---

# Security

* Secure authentication
* Role-based access control
* Audit logging
* Encryption in transit
* Password security
* Backup and recovery

---

# Operations

* Incident reporting
* System monitoring
* Disaster recovery
* Business continuity
* Change management

---

# Financial

* Payment records
* Refund records
* Digital receipts
* Financial reporting

---

# Status Tracking

Every requirement shall be marked as:

* Not Started
* In Progress
* Implemented
* Verified
* Released
