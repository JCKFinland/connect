# CONNECT Project Charter

## Project Name

CONNECT – Finnish Intelligent Taxi Mobility Platform

---

## Project Purpose

The purpose of CONNECT is to design and develop a modern, secure, scalable, and regulation-aware taxi ride-hailing platform for Finland.

The platform will support passengers, taxi drivers, fleet operators, dispatchers, and system administrators while emphasizing operational efficiency, passenger safety, data security, and maintainability.

---

## Mission Statement

To build a trusted digital mobility platform that simplifies taxi transportation through modern technology while supporting high standards of safety, transparency, and operational excellence.

---

## Project Objectives

* Develop a production-ready taxi ride-hailing platform.
* Build independent applications for customers, drivers, and administrators.
* Provide efficient ride booking and dispatch services.
* Support fleet management and manual dispatch operations.
* Produce comprehensive operational reporting and analytics.
* Design for compliance with applicable Finnish transport regulations and other relevant legal obligations.
* Build an extensible architecture capable of supporting future mobility services.

---

## Scope

The initial release of CONNECT includes:

* Customer Application
* Driver Application
* Admin Portal
* Booking System
* Dispatch System
* Pricing Engine
* Driver Management
* Vehicle Management
* Fleet Management
* Reporting
* Notifications
* Payment Integration
* Digital Receipts
* Compliance Management
* Taximeter Integration

---

## Out of Scope (Initial Release)

* Food delivery
* Parcel logistics
* Public transport integration
* International expansion
* Autonomous vehicle support

These may be considered in future releases.

---

## Primary Stakeholders

* Passengers
* Taxi Drivers
* Fleet Operators
* Dispatch Operators
* System Administrators
* Business Owners
* Regulatory Authorities (where applicable)
* Payment Service Providers

---

## Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

* The platform is production ready.
* Core ride-hailing workflows operate reliably.
* Security and privacy controls are implemented.
* Comprehensive documentation is complete.
* The platform supports operational monitoring and reporting.
* The architecture is maintainable and scalable.

---

## Project Principles

* Documentation before implementation.
* Quality over speed.
* Security by design.
* Maintainability by design.
* Compliance by design.
* Test-driven validation where practical.
* Continuous improvement.

---

## Development Methodology

CONNECT will follow an iterative, milestone-based development process.

Each milestone will include:

* Requirements
* Architecture
* Database updates
* Backend implementation
* Frontend implementation
* Testing
* Documentation
* Review
