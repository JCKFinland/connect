# CONNECT Vision

## Vision Statement

CONNECT aims to become Finland's leading intelligent taxi mobility platform by providing a secure, reliable, transparent, and regulation-aware ecosystem for passengers, drivers, fleet operators, and dispatchers.

The platform will leverage modern software engineering, scalable cloud architecture, and data-driven decision making to improve the efficiency and quality of taxi transportation.

---

# Long-Term Vision

CONNECT will evolve beyond a ride-hailing application into a comprehensive mobility platform capable of supporting:

* Taxi ride-hailing
* Fleet management
* Corporate transportation
* Airport transfers
* Scheduled rides
* Accessible transportation
* Driver and vehicle compliance management
* Business intelligence and analytics
* Future mobility services
